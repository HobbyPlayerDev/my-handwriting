## Hershey fonts

The "standard handwriting" for characters without own samples (`js/fallback-font.js`,
`skill/my-handwriting/scripts/fallback_font.json`, built by `tools/build_fallback_font.py` from
`tools/hershey/futural.jhf`) is converted from the Hershey Roman Simplex font. Characters that the
font does not contain (€ § ß « » „ “ … ≤ √ and others) were drawn for this project.

Acknowledgements required by the Hershey font license (full text in `tools/hershey/LICENSE-hershey.txt`):

- The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working at the
  U. S. National Bureau of Standards.
- The format of the Font data in this distribution was originally created by
  James Hurt, Cognition, Inc., 900 Technology Park Drive, Billerica, MA 01821.

The data is stored here in its own JSON/JavaScript format (not the format distributed by the U.S. NTIS).
